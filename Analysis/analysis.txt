System Actors:
- Student
- Clinic Staff
- Administrator

Functional Requirements:
Student Requirements
01 Students can register an account.
02 Students can log in securely.
03 Students can view available appointment slots by date and time.
04 Students can book an appointment.
05 Students can reschedule an existing appointment.
06 Students can cancel an appointment.
07 Students can view booking status.
08 Students can view appointment history.

Clinic Staff Requirements
01 Clinic staff can log in securely.
02 Clinic staff can view dashboard summaries of bookings and slot status.
03 Clinic staff can create doctor appointment slots.
04 Clinic staff can edit doctor appointment slots.
05 Clinic staff can delete doctor appointment slots.
06 Clinic staff can view student bookings.
07 Clinic staff can confirm appointments.
08 Clinic staff can reschedule appointments.
09 Clinic staff can cancel appointments.
10 Clinic staff can view daily and weekly clinic schedules.
11 Clinic staff can monitor patient booking records.

Administrator Requirements
01 Administrators can log in securely.
02 Administrators can view overall system statistics.
03 Administrators can manage student accounts.
04 Administrators can manage clinic staff accounts.
05 Administrators can add, edit, deactivate, or view user accounts.
06 Administrators can view appointment records.
07 Administrators can monitor system activity logs.

Non-Functional Requirements:
01 Usability
The system shall provide a clear and easy-to-use interface for students, clinic staff, and administrators.
02 Security
The system shall support secure login and protect user account information.
03 Responsiveness
The interface shall work properly on both desktop and mobile devices.
04 Performance
Main pages such as login, slot viewing, and booking pages shall load within a reasonable time under normal conditions.
05 Availability Visibility
Appointment slot status and booking status shall be displayed clearly to reduce confusion.
06 Consistency
The interface shall use consistent colours, layout, buttons, and navigation across all pages.
07 Reliability
Booking records, appointment status, and user data shall remain accurate and organised.
08 Scalability
The system should support multiple users accessing appointment services during normal university usage periods.
