This university clinic appointment system prototype aims to demonstrate key user interactions and system workflows for three target user roles: students, clinic staff, and administrators.
It focuses on core appointment management processes, enabling users to experience how to create, view, update, and monitor appointments within a streamlined web environment.

For students
The prototype includes pages for registration, login, viewing available appointment slots, making appointments, managing existing appointments, and viewing appointment history.

For clinic staff
The prototype provides interfaces for managing available doctor slots, confirming or updating student appointments, and viewing clinic schedules and patient appointment records.

For administrators
The prototype includes dashboard pages for managing user accounts, monitoring appointment records, and viewing overall system activity.

The prototype employs a clear and user-friendly interface design, ensuring each role can easily access the functions relevant to their responsibilities. 
While it is a simplified version of the complete system, it effectively demonstrates the intended structure, navigation flow,
and key operational features of the proposed appointment platform.
